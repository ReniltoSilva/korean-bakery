About us

We started this bakery with one simple dream: to bring the warm, comforting flavors of Korea to Hanoi. Every bun, cake, and pastry is made from scratch using traditional Korean recipes passed down through generations — with a little love added at every step.


Our ingredients are sourced fresh daily, and everything is baked in-house so you always get the softest, most flavorful treat possible. Whether you're a longtime fan of Korean food or trying it for the first time.

In Hanoi, the best value cookie spot that best suits Korean palates is truly Bonado. While most high-quality and delicious dessert cookies cost between 50,000 and 60,000 VND, the ones here are around 20,000 VND, and personally, I think this price range is actually justified. (I assume the high prices are mostly due to rent, but even so...) Nevertheless, the taste is truly better than any other bakery. Of course, their other breads and cakes are also quite good value, but this is the number one place for cookies. They are closed on Sundays, so whenever I go early on a Saturday, I buy a huge amount and stock it in the freezer to eat.
